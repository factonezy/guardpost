GuardPost Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

GuardPost ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Email Addresses

When you use the Breach Check feature, we process email addresses to check against the Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) database. We do NOT store these email addresses on our servers.

Account Information

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Encrypted password (via Firebase Authentication)

Subscription Data

Purchase information for premium subscriptions is processed through RevenueCat/Stripe. We do not store credit card information.

Device Information

We may collect:

  • Device model and OS version
  • App version
  • Crash reports (for debugging)

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide breach checking services
  • To send security alerts (if you subscribe to premium)
  • To improve app performance
  • To process subscriptions

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed and encrypted
  • No plain-text storage of credentials

Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from notifications anytime

Data Retention

  • Free accounts: Data retained until account deletion
  • Premium accounts: Data retained during active subscription + 30 days
  • Breach check results are temporary and not permanently stored

Children's Privacy

GuardPost is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Users will be notified of material changes via the app.
